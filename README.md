# Catch-the-Heart Challenge ❤

Welcome to the **Catch-the-Heart Challenge**, a fun and fast-paced game that tests your reflexes and precision! Can you catch the elusive red heart before it breaks yours?

## How to Play
1. Move your mouse to control the **yellow paddle**.  
2. Catch the **red heart** to score points.  
3. Be quick! The heart gets faster with every bounce!  

💔 **Miss it**, and the game will break your heart—literally!  

---

## Features
- Interactive paddle control using the mouse.  
- Increasing difficulty to keep you on your toes.  
- Simple, yet addictive gameplay.  

---

## Pro Tips
- Stay focused—each bounce makes the heart harder to catch.  
- Perfect your timing and paddle placement for a high score.
